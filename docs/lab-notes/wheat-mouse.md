# Wheat Mouse

During the first live Guardian run, Hermes' Nous-free clarification timed out
and Apple on-device inference completed the explanation instead. While talking
through that graceful fallback, speech-to-text transformed “Nous timed out”
into the entirely confident phrase “Wheat Mouse timed out.”

Wheat Mouse now names the small but serious principle behind the fallback
chain: an explanation provider may fail, stall, or become ridiculous without
ever acquiring authority over the protected file. Guardian preserves the
approved bytes, keeps the proposal pending, and leaves the decision with the
human.

The product survived its first real Hermes wizard. Wheat Mouse survives on the
lab wall.
